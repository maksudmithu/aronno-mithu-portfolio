FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how the service works, what you receive and how we start.

Who is this service for?

Any business in the USA or Canada whose growth depends on new leads and booked appointments. If you need new clients, and you want a steady flow of enquiries and calls, the system is built for you.

How does the Facebook lead generation funnel work?

I create and optimize your Facebook Business Page, build a free guide PDF for your niche, and launch a Facebook and Instagram lead ad offering that guide. When someone submits the lead form, automation instantly emails them the guide download link plus your meeting link. If they do not reply, follow-up emails are sent every 2 days.

Do I need to already have a Facebook Business Page?

No. Creating and professionally optimizing your Facebook Business Page is part of the service. If you already have one, I will audit and optimize it before launching your campaign.

Is the LinkedIn outreach really manual?

Yes. I work from your own LinkedIn account. I build your targeted audience list with Sales Navigator, send invitations, message people after they accept, manage replies and follow up. Your message template and sequence are written with you, based on your niche and audience. Nothing generic is sent in your name.

What information is in a cold email prospect list?

Each prospect includes their name, email address, company and the other key details you need, researched and segmented so the outreach is relevant. Interested replies are handed to you directly, and everyone else receives planned follow-up emails.

Can I order only an Animation Ad or a Motion Graphics Ad, without a campaign?

Yes. You can order an Animation Ad or a Motion Graphics Ad on its own and receive the finished video file. If you want, I can also launch the ad for you on Facebook. That choice is yours.

What does the monthly content service include?

30 content posts and 10 Reels videos per month, planned in a monthly content calendar, written, designed, scheduled and published for you. It keeps your social profiles active and builds the brand awareness and identity that make your ads and outreach convert better.

How fast can we launch?

After our free call and an agreed plan, your first campaign, funnel or outreach system is built and launched within 7 days, with tracking and automation in place from the start.

How much does it cost?

Packages are tailored to your niche, channels and workload, so pricing is confirmed on a free call. There are no hidden costs. You approve the plan and the price before any work begins.
